Privacy Policy

Last updated November 9, 2026

Orderly is a shared task app. This page explains what we store about you, why, who can see it, and what you can do about it. We don’t sell your data and we don’t show ads.

What we collect

Account details. Your username and your password. We never store the password itself, only a salted Argon2 hash of it.

Email address (optional). If you add one, we keep it, and we email you a 6-digit code to confirm it. You only need an email for password resets, reminders and digests.

What you put in Orderly. Projects, lists, tasks, notes, deadlines, priorities, repeat rules, assignments, and the order you arrange things in.

Sharing information. Who belongs to which project or list, the permissions they were given, and pending or past invites, including who sent them.

Settings. Your time zone and your calendar and email preferences, such as digest time, reminder timing and which tasks are included.

Calendar link. If you use the calendar subscription link, we store a secret token that identifies your feed.

Connected calendars. If you connect Google Calendar or Outlook, we store the access and refresh tokens that let Orderly keep a calendar in your account up to date, plus a record of which tasks have been synced.

Email records. A log of which digests and reminders were already sent, so nothing is sent twice.

We don’t ask for your real name, phone number, address or payment details.

How we use it

  • To run the app: sign you in, save your tasks, and keep shared lists in sync live for everyone in them.
  • To send the emails you asked for: verification and reset codes, invites, assignment notes, due-date reminders and daily digests. Each can be turned off in Settings.
  • To put your tasks on your calendar through the subscription link or a connected calendar.
  • To keep the service secure and fix problems.

We don’t use your content for advertising, and we don’t sell or rent your information.

Who can see your tasks

  • People you share with. Everyone in a shared project or list can see its tasks, notes, assignments and each other’s usernames. What each person can do depends on the permissions the owner gave them.
  • Invite links. Anyone who opens an invite link can see who invited them and the name of the project or list, even before they sign in.
  • Your calendar link. Anyone who has your calendar subscription URL can read the tasks it includes. Treat it like a password and don’t post it publicly.

Anything you write in a shared list is visible to its members, so keep private things in a list you haven’t shared.

Services we rely on

We use a few outside services to operate Orderly. They process data only as needed to provide that service.

  • Gmail (Google). Our emails are sent through a Gmail account, so your email address and the message content pass through Google’s mail servers.
  • Google Calendar and Microsoft Outlook. Only if you choose to connect them. See the next section.
  • Hosting and connection. The website is served as static pages, and the app talks to our server over an encrypted connection that passes through a tunneling service. Those providers can see standard connection details such as IP addresses.
  • Google Fonts. The welcome page loads its typeface from Google, which receives your IP address when it does.
  • Todoist import. If you import from Todoist, the API token you paste is used for that import and is not stored.

Google and Microsoft data

Connecting a calendar is optional. When you connect Google Calendar, Orderly asks only for permission to create and manage calendars that Orderly itself creates. It can’t see your other calendars. For Outlook, it asks for permission to read and write calendar events, and it uses that to maintain a separate calendar named “Orderly”.

We use this access only to create, update and delete the events that mirror your tasks. We don’t use it for advertising, we don’t sell it, and we don’t share it with anyone else.

Orderly’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can disconnect at any time from Orderly’s settings. We then delete the stored tokens and try to remove the Orderly calendar from your account. You can also revoke access from your Google or Microsoft account settings.

Cookies and browser storage

  • authToken: keeps you signed in. It expires after 30 days or when you sign out.
  • theme: remembers your light or dark choice.
  • Temporary session storage in your browser holds an invite link or a short notice while you move between pages. It disappears when you close the tab.

These are needed for the app to work. We don’t use advertising cookies.

How long we keep data

We keep your account and content while your account exists. Deleting a task, list or project removes it. Verification and reset codes expire after 15 minutes.

To delete your account and everything in it, email support.orderly.team@gmail.com from the address on the account, or include your username. We’ll delete it within 30 days. Tasks in lists you shared with others stay with those lists unless an owner removes them.

Security

Passwords are hashed, reset and verification codes are stored hashed, expire quickly and lock after a few wrong tries, and changing your password through reset signs out other devices. Traffic between your browser and Orderly is encrypted.

No system is perfectly secure, so use a password you don’t use anywhere else. If you think your account was accessed by someone else, reset your password and let us know.

Your choices

  • Skip adding an email, or remove it.
  • Turn off any email type in Settings.
  • Choose which tasks appear on your calendar and in emails.
  • Disconnect Google Calendar or Outlook whenever you like.
  • Ask us what we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it, using the email below.

Children

Orderly isn’t for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has made an account, tell us and we’ll remove it.

Changes and contact

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we’ll update the date above and, where we can, let you know in the app or by email.

Questions or requests: support.orderly.team@gmail.com
